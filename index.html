Payment Options

Flexible Payment Options for Every Ratepayer

Card and direct debit options, with payment frequencies that suit different budgets and preferences.

Card Payments

Visa and Mastercard accepted. One-off or scheduled recurring card payments to the council.

Direct Debit

Bank account direct debit with ratepayer authorisation. Automated and reliable ongoing payments.

One-Off Payments

Pay the full balance or a partial amount at any time, without setting up a recurring schedule.

Scheduled Recurring

Set a schedule and payments process automatically. Ratepayers can update or cancel at any time.

Multiple Frequencies

Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, or a custom amount. Configurable to align with council policy.

Two-Portal Platform

One platform. Two dedicated portals.

Separate, purpose-built portals for ratepayers and council staff — each designed for their specific needs.

01
Ratepayer Portal

Designed for the ratepayer.

Everything a ratepayer needs to manage how they pay — in one simple, self-service space.

  • View current rates balance and account details
  • Set up card or direct debit payment
  • Choose payment frequency and schedule
  • Update payment method at any time
  • View payment history and upcoming payments
  • Receive automated payment reminders
  • Access from any device, at any time
02
Council Admin Portal

Designed for the rates team.

A control centre for revenue teams — full visibility and the tools to manage it all.

  • View and search all ratepayer accounts
  • Monitor active payment schedules
  • Track failed and upcoming payments
  • Manage card expiry and dishonours
  • Configure payment rules and settings
  • Generate reports for reconciliation
  • Maintain audit-friendly activity records

How It Works

Up and Running in Four Steps

From council onboarding to ratepayer self-service — a straightforward path to modern rates payments.

1
Step 01

Council Onboarding

Configure branding, payment rules, data integration, and schedule options. FlexiRates is set up to align with each council's requirements.

2
Step 02

Ratepayer Access

Ratepayers access the portal from the council website, a link in their rates notice, or an email — whichever is most convenient.

3
Step 03

Payment Setup

Ratepayers select their preferred payment option, set up card or direct debit details, and confirm their payment schedule online.

4
Step 04

Ongoing Management

Ratepayers manage their account online. The council monitors activity through the admin portal and receives reporting for reconciliation.
