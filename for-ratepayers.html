Getting Started

How FlexiRates Works for You

Getting set up is straightforward. Access the portal from your rates notice or council website, and you are in control from there.

1

Receive Your Notice

Your council rates notice will include a link for the FlexiRates portal. You can also access it directly from your council's website.

2

Access the Portal

Sign in securely to view your rates account, current balance, and any existing payment arrangements. No new account to create — your details are linked from your rates record.

3

Set Up Payments

Choose your preferred payment method — card or direct debit — and select a frequency that suits you. Review and confirm your schedule before it begins.

4

Manage Online

Update your payment method, view upcoming payments, and manage your account any time through the portal. No need to call council.

Portal Capabilities

What You Can Do with FlexiRates

A self-service portal for managing your rates payments — at a time that suits you.

View Your Balance

Payment Options

Flexible Payment Options

FlexiRates offers payment options designed to reduce the financial pressure of large quarterly rates bills. Choose the method and frequency that works for you.

  • Visa and Mastercard
    Pay by card with a secure, one-time card setup. Use for scheduled recurring payments or a one-off payment at any time.
  • Bank Account Direct Debit
    Authorise payments directly from your bank account. Funds are debited automatically on your chosen schedule.
  • Scheduled Recurring Payments
    Set up a payment schedule and payments process automatically. You can update or cancel your schedule at any time.
  • Multiple Frequencies
    Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, or a custom amount — subject to the options your council makes available.

Your Security

Your Information is Secure

FlexiRates is designed to protect your payment details and personal information. Here is how.

Card Details Kept Secure

Your card details are processed through a PCI DSS compliant system. Card numbers are never stored by council — only a secure token is kept for future payments.

Secure Authentication

Access to your rates account is protected by secure authentication. Only you can access your payment portal and account details.

Encrypted Transmission

All information transmitted between your browser and the portal is encrypted. Your data is protected in transit and at rest.

Direct Debit Authorisation

Direct debit payments require your explicit authorisation. You authorise each direct debit arrangement before any payments are processed.

Privacy-Aware Design

Your personal information is handled in accordance with Australian privacy principles. FlexiRates is designed to collect only what is necessary.

Payment Records Available

A complete record of your payments is available in the portal at any time. View your full payment history whenever you need it.
