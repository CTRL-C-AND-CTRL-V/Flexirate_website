About FlexiRates

Helping Australian councils modernise rate payments.

FlexiRates is a rate payment platform designed for Australian local government, powered by Bill Buddy. Our mission is to make rate payments more flexible for ratepayers while making rates management more efficient for council teams.

Request a Demo

Who We Are

We're on a mission to make council rates easier — for everyone involved.

Paying council rates is one of the least flexible bills Australians face, and managing those payments is one of the more manual jobs council teams handle. We're an Australian team that set out to make both sides of that experience better.

"Rates shouldn't be the hardest bill to pay — or the hardest to manage."

The idea behind FlexiRates

Our Mission

Modern Rate Payments for Every Australian Council

Ratepayer First

We believe ratepayers deserve better than a single large annual bill. FlexiRates gives people more control over how they manage rates within their budget.

Practical for Councils

FlexiRates is designed around the reality of how council rates teams work — reducing workload while improving visibility, not adding complexity.

Secure and Trusted

Security and compliance are not afterthoughts. FlexiRates is designed to meet the expectations of Australian local government for secure payment handling.

Powered by Bill Buddy

Backed by 20+ years of Australian payment expertise.

FlexiRates is powered by Bill Buddy, an Australian payment provider that has spent more than two decades handling recurring and scheduled payments for organisations across the country.

That experience runs underneath everything FlexiRates does — card processing, direct debit, and the platform the ratepayer and council portals are built on. We pair it with a design made specifically for council rates.
