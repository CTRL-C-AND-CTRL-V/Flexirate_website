The Challenge

Rates teams are still doing too much by hand.

Most Australian councils still rely on paper forms, phone calls, and manual data entry to manage rate payments — putting pressure on staff and ratepayers alike.

Too many calls.

Ratepayers ring for balances, plan changes, and updates that should be self-service.

Paper everywhere.

Each payment arrangement is a form to chase, sign, scan, and key into the ledger.

No clear view.

Hard to tell which plans are active, which payments failed, and what's due next.

Failed payments pile up.

Dishonours, expired cards, and bounced debits all need manual chasing.

Quarterly bill shock.

Big lump-sum bills frustrate ratepayers who want to spread the cost out.

Reconciliation drains hours.

Matching payments across channels back to the rates ledger eats up team time.

FlexiRates is built to take these tasks off your team.

Admin Portal

Council Admin Portal Features

A purpose-built dashboard giving rates teams the visibility and tools they need.

Account Monitoring

View and search all ratepayer accounts, see current balances, active payment schedules, and account status at a glance.

Schedule Management

View all active payment schedules, upcoming payment dates, and schedule history. Understand the forward payment pipeline at any time.

Failed Payment Visibility

Immediately identify failed and dishonoured payments. See which accounts need follow-up and take action from the admin portal.

Card Expiry Management

Identify accounts with expiring or expired card details. Ratepayers can update their own card details online, reducing manual follow-up.

Reporting and Reconciliation

Generate payment reports to support reconciliation of the rates ledger. Export data for use in financial management systems.

Audit Logs

Detailed records of payment activity, authorisations, and account changes. Supports compliance, audit, and dispute resolution requirements.

Configurable Settings

Configure payment options, frequencies, and rules to align with your council's rates policy. Adjust settings without technical intervention.

Ratepayer Support Tools

Look up accounts, view payment history, and assist ratepayers with enquiries directly from the admin portal without needing separate systems.

Role-Based Access Control

Control which staff members have access to which admin functions. Supports least-privilege access principles and council governance requirements.

Security

Security Controls for Local Government

FlexiRates is designed with the security and governance requirements of Australian local government in mind. Payment processing is handled through a PCI DSS compliant environment, and council admin access is controlled and logged.

  • Role-based access control for admin portal users
  • Audit trails for all payment and account activity
  • Secure ratepayer authentication and session management
  • PCI DSS compliant payment processing capability
  • Secure card tokenisation — no raw card data stored by council
  • Direct debit authorisation support with digital records
  • Privacy-aware design aligned with Australian privacy principles
PCI DSS Compliant
Payment card processing through a PCI DSS compliant environment. Council does not handle raw card data.
Secure Data Handling
Sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest. System design follows least-privilege principles.
Audit-Friendly Records
Complete activity logs for payment events, authorisations, and admin actions support audit and compliance requirements.
Role-Based Access
Granular access controls ensure staff can only access the functions required for their role.
